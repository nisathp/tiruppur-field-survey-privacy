Privacy Policy for Tiruppur Field Survey

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Tiruppur Field Survey is a field-survey application used by authorised surveyors and supervisors to collect and manage survey records. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Information we collect

The information collected depends on the fields completed by an authorised surveyor. It can include:

  • Surveyor account details, including name, field ID, assigned ward, role, login time, and logout time.
  • Household and survey details entered in the form, which may include names, addresses, contact details, demographic information, and responses to survey questions.
  • Precise device location (latitude, longitude, and the corresponding address) when a surveyor chooses to capture location for a survey.
  • Voice recordings when a surveyor records and attaches audio to a survey.
  • Operational information such as survey submission time, selected sync destination, and sync status.

How we use information

We use the information solely to create, review, administer, synchronise, and report authorised field-survey records; manage surveyor access and payments where configured; and resolve sync or support issues.

Storage and sharing

Survey data is first stored on the surveyor's device so that work can continue offline. When the surveyor starts a sync, the selected data is uploaded to the Google Sheets and Google Drive destinations configured by the supervising organisation. Audio files may be stored in the configured Google Drive folder.

Only the organisation, its authorised supervisors, surveyors, and administrators of the configured Google accounts should be granted access to these records. We do not sell survey data or use it for advertising.

Location and microphone permissions

Location permission is used only to capture a survey location when the surveyor requests it. Microphone permission is used only when the surveyor records audio for a survey. A surveyor may decline these permissions, although the related survey feature will not work.

Retention and deletion

Records remain in the configured Google Sheets and Drive folders until the supervising organisation deletes them or its retention rules require deletion. Removing a survey from the device does not automatically remove a previously synced copy from Google services. For a deletion request, contact the supervising organisation or the contact below.

Security

We use access controls provided by the configured Google accounts and expect administrators to limit Sheet and Drive access to authorised personnel. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure; administrators should avoid public sharing of survey folders and recordings.

Children

This app is an internal field-work tool and is not intended for use by children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be available at this page.
